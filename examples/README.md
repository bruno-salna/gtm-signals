# Examples

**Everything in this folder is made up.** "Examplia" (`XX`) is a fictional country, and every company, number, link and domain is invented. The files show the shape of the work, not real data.

The setting continues the example from the ICP + tiering guide: a company sells production-planning software to food producers, has 36 customers matched to the market, and two people who can work about 80 new accounts in the next month. The list is that guide's tiered Examplia market map. Signals were checked on 2026-11-05.

- [signal-plan.md](signal-plan.md): the filled-in signal plan from steps 2, 3 and 5
- [list-signals.csv](list-signals.csv): a few rows with every `signal_` column added
- [signals.csv](signals.csv): the signals behind those rows, one per line
- [signals-summary.md](signals-summary.md): the one-page summary for the whole list

The rows show the common cases: a tier 1 company with a new leader (`now`), one with two signals, one with staff growth only (`soon`) and a blocked careers page, a tier 2 company with a new site, a tier 3 lost deal with a strong signal (flagged, not moved), a current customer with an expansion cue, a customer's signal from before it started (used in the test), a row with no headcount filed, and rows with nothing found.
